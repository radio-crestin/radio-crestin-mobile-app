**Privacy Policy**

Radio Crestin is a free, open-source app by Nicolae Iosif for listening to Romanian Christian radio
stations. This page says what data the app sends off your device, why, and who processes it. It is the
privacy policy linked from Google Play and the App Store.

**No account, no personal details**

The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, phone number or contacts. Everything
below is tied to a random ID the app creates when it is installed, not to who you are.

**What the app sends, and why**

- **A random device ID and a push notification token.** The ID tells one installation apart from
  another, for example so a station review can be edited later. The token (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  lets us send notifications about stations; you can turn them off in the app or in your device settings.
  With them we send basic device facts: platform, operating system version, device model, app version,
  language and time zone.
- **How the app is used.** Which stations and screens you open, playback events (play, pause, buffering,
  errors), songs you like, and the searches you type in the station search. On a share of devices where
  an error happens, a short recording of the app's screens (session replay) helps us see what went wrong.
  We use all of this to keep playback working and to improve the app.
- **Crash reports and diagnostic logs.** When the app crashes or an error occurs, it sends the error and
  the technical logs around it. From the Settings screen you can also send us your device's logs yourself.
- **Approximate location.** The app never reads your GPS or location. Our analytics service estimates
  your country and city from your IP address, so we know where the app is used.
- **Station reviews you write.** If you rate a station, the stars and the message you typed are sent to
  us and may be shown to other listeners. Writing a review is optional.

**Who processes the data**

- **Radio Crestin's own servers** (`api.radiocrestin.ro`) store station data, reviews, likes and the
  device registration.
- **PostHog** processes analytics, crash reports, logs and session replays on our behalf, through our
  address `k.radiocrestin.ro`, on servers in the European Union.
- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** delivers push notifications.

These services act on our behalf only. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to
show ads.

**Security**

All of this data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

**Data stored on your device**

Your favorite stations, settings and cache stay on your device. Settings → "Șterge datele aplicației"
deletes them.

**Deleting your data**

To have the data linked to your device deleted from our servers, write to contact@radiocrestin.ro.
Uninstalling the app stops all collection.

**Children**

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

**Changes to this policy**

When this policy changes, the new version is published on this page.
