# Radio Crestin 1.5.0

**Released:** May 2, 2026 (GitHub prerelease, build 83) · **Changes since:** 1.3.3 (build 67) \
**Platforms:** iPhone, iPad, Android, Android TV, Apple TV, Mac, Windows, CarPlay, Android Auto

<!-- say: Radio Crestin version one point five brings the app to the T V, to Chromecast and AirPlay speakers, and to Windows, and lets you like the songs you hear. -->

Radio Crestin comes to the big screen and to every speaker in the house: Android TV and Apple TV apps,
Chromecast and AirPlay, a Windows app, and likes for the songs you hear.

## Highlights

### TV apps

<!-- say: There is now a T V app. On Android T V and Google T V, with your favorite stations on the T V's home screen. And a native Apple T V app, with the station grid, Now Playing, and song history. -->

- **Android TV and Google TV:** a TV layout made for the remote, and your favorite stations as a channel on the
  TV's home screen.
- **Apple TV:** a native app with the station grid, Now Playing with previous/next, recent songs, ratings,
  likes and a QR code to share a station.

### Chromecast and AirPlay

<!-- say: Send the radio to a Chromecast, a Google speaker, or an AirPlay speaker. Playback starts on its own when you connect, and the T V shows the station and the song. -->

- Cast to Chromecast and Google Home speakers, with a Radio Crestin screen on the TV showing the station and song.
- AirPlay to HomePod, Apple TV and other speakers.
- Playback starts by itself when you connect; the device picker can link a TV app by code.

### Like the songs you hear

<!-- say: Like or dislike the song playing, on the phone, in the notification, in CarPlay and in Android Auto. Stations get ratings, and a song history shows what played. -->

- 👍 and 👎 on the phone's player, the notification, CarPlay and Android Auto.
- Station ratings and reviews; a song history with the times songs played.
- **Pentru tine** sorts the stations you are likely to want first.

### Windows and the desktop

<!-- say: Radio Crestin now runs on Windows, and the desktop apps get their own layout: a grid of stations with category tabs, and a player at the bottom. -->

- A Windows app, and a desktop layout on Mac and Windows: station grid with category tabs, search,
  and a mini player that opens into Now Playing.

### Steadier playback

<!-- say: For weak connections there is a new mode, Unstable connection, and you can choose to listen two or five minutes behind live, so short drops go unheard. -->

- **Conexiune instabilă** mode and a choice to listen 2 or 5 minutes behind live (**Încărcare în avans**).
- Song titles follow HLS audio precisely; iPhone reconnects after network changes instead of stopping.
- Android Auto shows the recently played queue; CarPlay gets like/dislike and a recently played list.
