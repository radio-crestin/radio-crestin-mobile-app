**Released:** May 5, 2026 (GitHub prerelease, build 84) · **Changes since:** [1.5.0](v1.5.0.md) \
**Platforms:** iPhone, iPad, Android, Android TV, Apple TV, Mac, Windows, CarPlay, Android Auto

<!-- say: Radio Crestin version one point five point one keeps the song on screen in step with what you hear, and uses less data while doing it. -->

A small follow-up to 1.5.0: the song on screen keeps in step with what you hear, with less data.

## Improvements

<!-- say: Song titles now change when the song you hear changes, checking every ten seconds while you listen and once a minute otherwise. The cast button shows again on iPhone. -->

- Song titles follow the audio: the app checks for changes every 10 s while you listen, once a minute
  otherwise, and fetches only what changed instead of the whole list.
- The cast button shows on iPhone again, and discovery restarts when you tap it.
- Turning **Conexiune instabilă** off goes back to a 2-minute **Încărcare în avans**; installs that were
  stuck on 5 minutes are reset to 2 once.

## Under the hood

<!-- slides: skip -->

- Apple TV builds are signed for the App Store and uploaded through TestFlight.
- iOS builds generate dSYMs for vendored frameworks; Xcode Cloud post-clone script added.
- fastlane scaffolding for TestFlight and the Play internal track.
