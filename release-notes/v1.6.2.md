# Radio Crestin 1.6.2

**Status:** not released yet · **Changes since:** [1.6.1](v1.6.1.md) \
**Platforms:** iPhone, iPad, Android, Android TV, Apple TV, Mac, Windows, CarPlay, Android Auto

## Highlights

<!-- say: News from Radio Creștin now shows in a card above the stations, on phones and tablets. Swipe between the announcements, tap one to open it, or hide it for good with the Ascunde button. A station can also have its own announcements: a bell on the player shows them while it plays. Some announcements are short polls or questionnaires, answered right in the app. Announcements can also arrive as notifications, now on iPhone too. -->

- **Announcements above the stations.** On phones and tablets, a card above the station list shows news
  from Radio Creștin: an event, a new feature, a scheduled maintenance. Swipe between them; tapping one opens
  a link, a page inside the app or a station, and "Ascunde" hides it for good on that phone. It takes the
  place of the share and CarPlay cards while there are announcements, and it opens with the stations, even
  on the first launch; news that arrives later slides in smoothly, without moving the announcement you are
  reading.
- **Announcements of the station you listen to.** While a station with its own announcements plays, a strip
  marked "nou" sits on top of the mini player, and a bell on the mini player and in the full player opens
  them.
- **Polls and questionnaires.** An announcement can open a short form in a sheet over the app, in the app's
  light or dark theme.
- **Announcements as notifications, on iPhone too.** A notification opens its announcement in the app. On
  iPhone the app now registers for notifications (it asked for permission but never got the token), and on
  Android 13 and later it asks once for permission. With the app open, the announcement slides down over
  the screen instead.

## Fixes

<!-- say: On Android, the app no longer freezes at launch, and the Back button no longer stops the radio. Stations with a single stream now start on a slow connection, each favorite is listed once, and a rare iPhone crash is gone. A YouTube playlist on a playlist station now plays. -->

- **On Android, the app no longer freezes at launch.** Versions before 1.6.2 kept every song and artist they
  had ever shown in a cache file that nothing read, and opened it at each launch; on a phone used for months,
  reading it froze the app long enough for Android to report it as not responding (0.27% of sessions in Play
  Console, up from 0.16%). The app no longer keeps that cache and deletes the old file. Android's records of
  any later freeze now reach the team at the next launch, with what the app was doing.
- **On Android, Back keeps the radio playing.** Leaving the app with the Back button on the stations list
  closed it, and the station stopped; the app now goes to the background, as with the Home button, and the
  station keeps playing. Android TV still closes the app on Back.
- **On iPhone, the app no longer closes while it plays when a song's cover can't be loaded.** The note it
  wrote about the missing cover could crash it when the cover's address had special characters.
- **The Favorite list shows each station once.** A heart pressed on the lock screen, in the car or on a
  widget for a station that was already a favorite listed it twice, and the list then drew itself wrong;
  the app now drops the second copy.
- **A station with a single stream starts on a slow connection.** On mobile data, a station with only one
  stream (such as Aripi Spre Cer) could take more than 5 seconds to start; the app gave up, showed a
  connection error and started it over from the beginning. It now waits up to 15 seconds.
- **On Android TV, the list shows the station you come back to.** Going back from the player could leave the
  station's card off the screen; the list now jumps to it.
- **On iPad held sideways, a keyboard's arrow and space keys scroll the station list** instead of doing
  nothing.
- **On iPhone, a station that iOS doesn't let start shows its play button again** instead of a pause button
  over silence, so it can be started with another tap.
- **YouTube playlists play.** On a playlist station, an item that is a whole YouTube playlist left the
  player grey and was marked as failed; the playlist now plays from its first video.

## Under the hood

<!-- slides: skip -->

For developers; nothing here changes what listeners see.

- iPhone's HLS hold proxy drops a connection the player closed before the proxy accepted it, instead of
  reporting an unhandled `OS Error: Invalid argument, errno = 22` from `HttpServer`.
- Android turns Flutter's own deep linking off (`flutter_deeplinking_enabled`), as iOS already did: the app
  opens its links with `app_links`, and Flutter also pushed each link as a named route the app doesn't have.
- Error tracking drops the lock-screen artwork cache's storage failures (a full or not yet readable disk):
  `flutter_cache_manager` opens its folder and database before anything listens, so they surfaced as
  unhandled errors, though the only effect is a lock screen without artwork.
- Announcements follow the shared announcements spec (backend, website, app). The list
  (`GET /announcements/`, no query parameters) is cached by Cloudflare and kept on the device with its ETag;
  filters (platform, locale, app version, country) are checked on the device. The list loads with the
  stations at startup (from the device at once, or from the API within 1.5 s on a first launch) and refreshes
  on every return to the app and on pull-to-refresh. The card keeps the API's order (`position`); `rotate` and
  `random` only change where each launch starts in it. With `settings.autoplay_seconds` the card moves on by
  itself, as tall as its tallest announcement; it waits while touched, scrolled away or in the background, and
  stops at the last one, after the listener pages or closes one, and with reduced motion. Events go to
  `POST /announcements/events/` and PostHog `announcement_*`; forms report `form_*`. Local state in
  SharedPreferences (`announcements_*`). Android channel `announcements`. Preview link
  `radiocrestin://anunturi-preview?token=…`. `webview_flutter` is now a direct dependency (forms).
- iOS: `aps-environment` entitlement added; the FCM token is taken after the APNs one and sent again once it
  arrives; `onTokenRefresh` re-registers on both platforms.
