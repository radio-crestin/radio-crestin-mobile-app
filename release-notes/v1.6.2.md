# Radio Crestin 1.6.2

**Status:** not released yet · **Changes since:** [1.6.1](v1.6.1.md) \
**Platforms:** iPhone, iPad, Android, Android TV, Apple TV, Mac, Windows, CarPlay, Android Auto

## Fixes

<!-- say: On Android, leaving the app with the Back button no longer stops the radio. The station keeps playing in the background, as it does when you go to the home screen. -->

- **On Android, Back keeps the radio playing.** Leaving the app with the Back button on the stations list
  closed it, and the station stopped; the app now goes to the background, as with the Home button, and the
  station keeps playing. Android TV still closes the app on Back.

<!-- say: A station item that is a whole YouTube playlist now plays. Before, its player stayed grey. -->

- **YouTube playlists play.** On a playlist station, an item that is a whole YouTube playlist left the
  player grey and was marked as failed; the playlist now plays from its first video.
