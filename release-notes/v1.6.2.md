# Radio Crestin 1.6.2

**Status:** not released yet · **Changes since:** [1.6.1](v1.6.1.md) \
**Platforms:** iPhone, iPad, Android, Android TV, Apple TV, Mac, Windows, CarPlay, Android Auto

## Fixes

<!-- say: On Android, leaving the app with the Back button no longer stops the radio. The station keeps playing in the background, as it does when you go to the home screen. -->

- **On Android, Back keeps the radio playing.** Leaving the app with the Back button on the stations list
  closed it, and the station stopped; the app now goes to the background, as with the Home button, and the
  station keeps playing. Android TV still closes the app on Back.

<!-- say: On Android phones that had used the app for a long time, opening it could freeze for several seconds, and Android offered to close it. The app now opens without waiting. -->

- **On Android, the app no longer freezes at launch.** Versions before 1.6.2 kept every song and artist they
  had ever shown in a cache file that nothing read, and opened it at each launch; on a phone used for months,
  reading it froze the app long enough for Android to report it as not responding (0.27% of sessions in Play
  Console, up from 0.16%). The app no longer keeps that cache and deletes the old file.
